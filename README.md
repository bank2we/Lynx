## Overview

**VOYAGE** is an interactive space exploration and space agency management game developed by **LYNX**.

The game puts players in charge of their own space agency, where they plan ambitious missions, design spacecraft, manage limited resources, and make critical decisions throughout their journey across the solar system.

Inspired by real-world space exploration and NASA data, VOYAGE combines strategic planning, spacecraft engineering, resource management, and interactive mission experiences into one immersive adventure.

## Key Features

- **Space Agency Management** — Establish and manage your own space agency.
- **Spacecraft Assembly** — Select and configure spacecraft components, engines, and equipment.
- **Mission Planning** — Plan exploration missions and determine the best routes and destinations.
- **Resource Management** — Balance budgets, spacecraft mass, power consumption, and available resources.
- **Interactive Missions** — Experience mission stages and respond to unexpected challenges.
- **Landing Operations** — Evaluate landing sites and manage associated risks.
- **Mission Evaluation** — Receive performance scores based on mission objectives and decisions.

## Gameplay

Every mission requires careful planning and strategic decisions.

Players are responsible for:

1. Selecting spacecraft components and equipment.
2. Managing spacecraft mass and power requirements.
3. Planning mission objectives and destinations.
4. Allocating the available mission budget.
5. Evaluating landing locations and potential risks.
6. Completing mission stages and reviewing final performance.

Every decision matters, and mission success depends on how effectively players manage their resources and overcome challenges.

## Project Vision

Our vision is to create an engaging experience that introduces players to the complexity of space exploration while encouraging strategic thinking, problem-solving, and curiosity about the universe.

VOYAGE aims to bridge the gap between gaming and real-world space exploration through interactive gameplay and science-inspired systems.
