## Asset layout

- `assets/characters/` — runtime character sprites
- `assets/backgrounds/` — runtime planetary art
- `assets/environments/` and `assets/tilesets/` — ready for scene expansion
- `assets/audio/sfx/` — curated runtime sound effects
- `assets/third_party/` — complete downloaded packs and original licenses

The Cozy Space archives supplied by the project owner are preserved unmodified under `assets/third_party/cozy-space/`, including their `README.txt` and `LICENSE.txt` files. Runtime copies selected for the game live in the other folders above.
